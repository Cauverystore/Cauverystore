# Google Stitch prompts — Cauvery Store

How to use: open https://stitch.withgoogle.com, sign in with Google, start a new project
(Web for desktop screens, App for phone screens). Paste the whole of DESIGN.md first, then
paste one prompt below. Generate one screen at a time.

## 1. Home page (Web)
Design the home page for Cauvery Store, an Indian online store for clothing and home goods,
following the design system above. Header with logo "Cauvery Store", search bar, account,
wishlist and cart icons. Split hero: left side headline "Everyday clothing and home goods,
delivered across India", one line "Prices include GST. Free returns within 7 days.", one green
button "Shop now"; right side a large photo of folded cotton sarees and towels. Then a
horizontal strip of category tiles (Sarees, Men's shirts, Kids, Bedsheets, Towels, Kitchen).
Then "New arrivals" product grid of 8 cards with rupee prices. Then a two-column band:
"Secure payments by UPI and cards" and "Shipped across India". Deep green footer.

## 2. Category / product listing (Web)
Category page "Cotton Sarees" for Cauvery Store. Breadcrumb, title with item count, left
filter column (Price range, Colour swatches, Fabric, Size, In stock only), sort dropdown top
right, 4-column product grid of 12 cards with rupee prices, struck old prices, gold stars,
wishlist hearts, and "Sale" badges on a few. Pagination at the bottom.

## 3. Product page (Web)
Product page for "Handloom cotton saree – green with gold border" priced ₹1,299 (was ₹1,599),
"Inclusive of all taxes". Images left with thumbnails; details right: rating, colour chips,
quantity stepper, green "Add to cart" and outlined "Buy now" buttons, pincode delivery check,
7-day return note. Tabs below: Description, Fabric & care, Reviews. Then "You may also like"
horizontal strip.

## 4. Cart and checkout (Web)
Cart page: list of 3 items with image, name, size, quantity stepper, price and remove link.
Right summary panel: subtotal, gift wrap ₹49 option, delivery, total "Inclusive of GST",
coupon field, green "Proceed to checkout" button. Checkout step: address form (name, phone,
pincode, address, city, state), then payment choice (UPI, cards, net banking) via Razorpay.

## 5. Mobile home (App)
Phone version of the Cauvery Store home page: top bar with logo and cart, search field
below, small hero card with one button, round category icons in a scroll row, 2-column
product grid, bottom tab bar (Home, Categories, Wishlist, Cart, Account).

## 6. Mobile product page (App)
Phone product page: swipeable image, name, price, "Inclusive of all taxes", colour chips,
size chips, delivery pincode check, collapsible Description / Reviews, sticky bottom bar
with price and green "Add to cart".
